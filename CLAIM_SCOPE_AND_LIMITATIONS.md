# Claim scope

- Inputs are independent, finite nonnegative e-variables. Precision here means output expectation one under every product law whose marginals have mean one.
- The construction uses the known Lugano Boolean selector and works for a hard cutoff or a continuous piecewise-linear cutoff. The continuous counterexample has three inputs. Both variants are nonmonotone.
- The excluded class averages final wealth from a fixed law of adaptive read-once strategies with fractions and initial capital in the unit interval. The four-point maximum-error lower bound of 3/52 applies to this class and its pointwise limits.
- The separate increasing-merger question and the two-input precise case lie outside the proved result. The paper does not claim optimality of the four-point lower bound.
- The statistical readout and its four-point certificate are the scope of the novelty assessment. The Boolean map and the general precision-to-admissibility argument have prior sources. [Literature audit](audits/public_safe_reports/LITERATURE_AUDIT.md)
