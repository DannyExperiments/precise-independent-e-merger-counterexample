# Statement and full proof

A continuous precise merger of three independent e-values is outside the generalized martingale mixtures defined by Vovk and Wang. Quantitatively, every mixture and every pointwise limit of mixtures incurs a maximum error of at least 3/52 on the four rational inputs in the manuscript. The exact merger therefore has no pointwise dominator within that class.

The [paper](../paper/manuscript.pdf) gives the proof for a continuous piecewise-linear cutoff and for a hard threshold. The [LaTeX source](../paper/manuscript.tex) is self-contained. The [literature audit](../audits/public_safe_reports/LITERATURE_AUDIT.md) discusses the known Lugano selector, prior admissibility result and separate monotone question.
